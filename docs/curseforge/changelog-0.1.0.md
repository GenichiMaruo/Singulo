## Singulo 0.1.0

First public release.

### Content
- Five stages of technology, from thermoelectric power to a rotating black hole reactor.
- Multiblock machines: cryogenic cooling tower, particle accelerator, degenerate compactor, C-Cavity, degenerate furnace, Penrose reactor, event horizon shield tower, T-Cylinder and wormhole generator.
- Four kinds of ruins with records, damaged components and the Horizon Warden boss.
- Gravity tools: Graviton Manipulator, black hole bombs and the gravity slingshot.
- Event horizon shields with player permits, time fields and wormhole logistics for power, items and fluids.
- Real-time gravitational lensing around black holes.
- In-game handbook, item tooltips, JEI support and a bundled 32×32 resource pack (Singulo HD).
- English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Korean translations, including the handbook and ancient records.

### Known issues
- Gravitational lensing with Iris shader packs has not been tested.

Please report bugs on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/GenichiMaruo/Singulo/issues).
