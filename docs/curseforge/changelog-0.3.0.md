## Singulo-0.3.0-1.21.1N

### Exploration and combat
- Expanded ruins with additional building materials, glass panels, redesigned vaults and boss rooms.
- Added Echo Sentinel and Gravity Remnant encounters, guardian cores, echo projectors, combat effects and boss sounds.
- Added meteorite craters, meteoric iron, stardust crystals and lightning-formed fulgurite.

### Machines and progression
- Added low- and high-gravity panels, gravity receivers and impact generators.
- Added the Keraunos discharge tower, deep-sea pressure collector and void collector, with related materials and recipes.
- Improved wormhole stabilization, mouths, ports and generator connections.
- Adjusted crafting costs and early thermoelectric output, reduced default time-crystal growth to 12 minutes, and allowed Hawking collection at the reactor's initial mass.

### Interface and resources
- Improved device displays, cable rendering, explorer compass targeting and multiblock definitions.
- Updated textures, models, handbook entries, recipe illustrations and Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Korean translations.
- Adopted the filename and display-name format `Singulo-<mod version>-<Minecraft version>N` for NeoForge builds.

This file is published with the **Beta** release type. Please report issues on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/GenichiMaruo/Singulo/issues).
