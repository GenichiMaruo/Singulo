## 26.1 weather fog versus lighting

Runtime diagnostics confirmed vanilla rainy-midnight environmental fog starts at -160 and ends at 768, unlike clear noon 0..1024. The resulting ~17.2% near-camera black fog exactly explained the standard-renderer floor reduction; GPU noon pixels, mesh light=15 and color=white were already correct. The development-only Rayleigh fixture now fixes environmental fog to 32..1024 in ALL phases so the nearby floor comparison isolates lighting. It does not change rain, time, lightmap input, production fog, comparison tolerances, or the power-off test. Standard and Sodium captures must be regenerated under this identical fixture condition.

Standard renderer run `review-rayleigh-standard8.log` passed on 2026-11-15: all runtime lightmap/AO/entity/power assertions, four lighting-isolated captures and one unmodified-rain-fog capture. The checker compared 84,000 floor pixels with zero RGB-channel differences for noon versus artificial noon and powered versus repowered. Power-off mean RGB drops were 187.7481 / 193.0958 / 194.0894. This verifies illumination equivalence under controlled fog, not removal of Minecraft's real rain fog.

The final optional-renderer run `review-rayleigh-optional5.log` also completed successfully after idempotent fixture cleanup: Sodium 241-value roundtrip/emissive checks, DH's four 256-texel checks, all lighting assertions, five captures, and 84,000-pixel exact comparison (zero differing RGB channels). Temporary GL/terrain diagnostic probes were then removed; only the documented review-only fog precondition remains.
